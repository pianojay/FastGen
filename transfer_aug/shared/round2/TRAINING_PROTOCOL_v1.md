# Round 2 learned transfer protocol — revision 1

## Question and invariants
Can scratch-trained unpaired translation shift the frozen A1 appearance score while preserving recognizable Pokémon identity, anatomy and markings? D0 is statistically softer/paler and D1 stronger in contrast/edges; neither is a semantic art-style label. Background replacement, anatomy damage, collapse or negligible change falsify useful transfer. This is mechanism exploration, not selection by generative FID or SiDA integration.
