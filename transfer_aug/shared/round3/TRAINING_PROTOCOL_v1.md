# Round3 multimodal transfer protocol — revision1

## Question and scope
Do reference-guided and sampled-style translation offer multiple plausible appearances while retaining recognizable identity, anatomy, markings and layout? Background inversion, damaged content, reference copying, collapsed diversity or variation dominated by noise are failure observations. The round2 appearance domains remain deliberately unchanged to compare mechanisms. They are statistical contrast/edge groups, not semantic style labels. A1 only, no A2/B, pretrained modules, learned external metrics, classifier/FID, SiDA integration or new dependencies. Scientific failure is a valid completed result.
