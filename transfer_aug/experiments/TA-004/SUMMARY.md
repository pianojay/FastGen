# TA-004: CUT D0→D1

Completed the frozen 20,000-update scratch-training contract on A1. CUT shifted the fixed appearance projection toward D1, but visible background replacement and marking damage prevent treating that shift as successful invariant-preserving augmentation.

Across **302 diagnostic translations**, median appearance-score shift was **+3.1752** and median RGB RMS change **0.18055** on a [0,1] scale; all 302 score shifts were positive. Mean output score was 1.7739 versus mean same-class D1 optimization score 1.9579. Median luminance-neighbor-gradient change was +0.02604. Median nearest same-class target optimization pixel RMS was 0.31225. These are descriptive statistics, not semantic or quality measures. There were no exact duplicate outputs or out-of-range float values; mean quantized boundary-channel fraction was 8.16%.

A small, unblinded AI review directly inspected all milestone sheets for **32 representative translations**, **32 target-identity controls**, and **eight separately selected high-RMS translation tails**. Broad source layouts often remained visible, with stronger contours, altered colors, halos/speckle and uncertain fine markings. Four representative translations visibly replaced pale backgrounds with black. All eight selected tails did this; Geodude lost solid arm/face structure and Arcanine lost stripes. Tail selection is intentionally biased toward large changes and may overlap representatives. These observations are not class accuracy or population failure prevalence.

The **302 target-identity controls** had median RMS 0.08990 and score shift +0.23185 (maximum RMS 0.75404); identity PatchNCE does not guarantee identity. Total final saved outputs: 604, with raw float arrays, PNGs and source/hash associations. Frozen descriptors/PCA use quantized viewing images; RGB/nearest-target RMS use raw generated floats mapped to [0,1].

The 9,061,060-parameter framework used **3,702.24 cumulative GPU seconds (1.0284 hours)**, including qualification/recovery and conservative preflight charges. Peak allocated memory was 2,569,035,776 bytes; reserved 3,472,883,712 bytes. GPU lease released. Qualification verified finite gradients/losses and exact GPU checkpoint recovery; final 32-image review regeneration matched quantized pixels exactly. Input hashes remained unchanged; diagnostics were excluded from optimization.

Evidence: `result.json`, `aggregate_metrics.json`, `visual_review.json`, `sheets/`, `training_complete.json`, and `gpu_resource_ledger.json`. Diagnostics participated in domain discovery, so are not independent validation. CUT's one-directional/contrastive objective differs from CycleGAN; compare D0→D1 as the common direction. No convergence, class-accuracy, generative-FID or downstream-usefulness claim. A next question is whether separately constrained background preservation reduces these failures; no successor run was launched.
