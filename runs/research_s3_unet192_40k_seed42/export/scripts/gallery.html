Development FID and interpretation

Lower FID is better. Each pass compares 3,020 generated images with 3,020 held-out official training images. Official validation was not used. Seeds 42 and 43 are sampling seeds for one trained model, not independent training replicates. Global FID does not measure requested-class accuracy or rule out memorization.
